The Sovereign Bandwidth & Compute Grid. Built from scratch to redirect digital wealth from monopolies straight into the pockets of everyday people.

## The Vision
Artificial Intelligence companies and tech conglomerates spend billions renting raw processing power from centralized server farms. Meanwhile, billions of smartphones sit completely idle in human pockets. Infinite X bridges this gap.
